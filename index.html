Methodology

How We Collected and Organized the Crime Information

This analysis is based on publicly available incident records from the San Francisco Open Data Portal, combining the historical crime dataset for 2003 to May 2018 with the more recent police incident dataset covering 2018 to the present.

Together, these records provide detailed information about each reported incident, including when it happened, where it happened, how it was classified, and which police and neighborhood areas were involved.

  • Date and time fields were used to analyze temporal patterns across the day.
  • Incident categories were used to compare the most common and most serious crime types.
  • District, neighborhood, and geographic information were used to support spatial interpretation.
  • Historical and recent datasets were aligned to create one long-run analytical view.
